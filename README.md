# 👾 Hey, I'm an Indie Game Developer!

🎮 **Indie Game Developer | Godot Engine | PSX Horror Enthusiast**

Hi! I'm an aspiring indie game developer from Kazakhstan, passionate about creating games, experimenting with gameplay mechanics, and bringing unusual ideas to life.

I mainly work with **Godot Engine**, focusing on horror games inspired by the PS1 era, low-poly graphics, retro aesthetics, and atmospheric storytelling. I love creating unsettling environments, experimenting with game mechanics, and finding ways to make simple concepts feel unique and memorable.

- 🎮 Develop games using **Godot Engine**
- 👁️ Create PSX-style and retro horror experiences
- 🧩 Experiment with gameplay mechanics and interactive systems
- 🌫️ Work on atmospheric environments, lighting, and immersion
- 📖 Develop game concepts, stories, and gameplay ideas
- 🔧 Build prototypes to test and improve new ideas

## 🚀 My Experience

I've worked on multiple Godot projects, including a paid game development project and my own game prototype.

I'm constantly experimenting with new ideas, learning new techniques, and improving my skills through hands-on development. I believe that even small projects can become something special with enough creativity and dedication.

## 🎃 Current Interests

## 🤝 Collaboration

I'm interested in collaborating with other creative people who share a passion for indie games.

I believe that a great horror game doesn't need realistic graphics or a massive budget. Sometimes, a strange sound in a dark hallway, an unsettling atmosphere, or a simple but clever mechanic can create an experience that stays with the player long after the game ends.

My goal is to keep learning, keep creating, and eventually turn my ideas into complete games that people can play and remember.
